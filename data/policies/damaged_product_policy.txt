# Damaged Product Policy
Document ID: POL-DMG-2025
Version: 1.4
Effective date: 2025-01-10
Owner: Quality and Claims

## 1. Reporting window
Visible transit damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Concealed damage, meaning damage discovered after unboxing, must be reported within 7 calendar days. Claims outside these windows are handled under the standard Return Policy instead, and lose the free-replacement entitlement.

## 2. Evidence required
A damage claim requires:

- At least three photographs: the outer packaging, the damaged area, and the shipping label
- The order number and the item serial number where present
- A short description of the damage

Claims lacking photographic evidence are held at status DMG-HOLD-01 until the customer supplies them. Claims are auto-closed after 14 days at this status.

## 3. Resolution options
Once a claim is validated the customer selects one of the following:

- Free replacement, shipped within 2 business days, subject to stock
- Full refund including all shipping charges
- Repair credit of up to 40 percent of the item price where the customer wishes to keep the item

Customers are never asked to pay return shipping for a validated damage claim.

## 4. Items that must not be returned
For hygiene and safety reasons, damaged items in the following categories are refunded or replaced without a physical return. The customer is asked to dispose of the item responsibly:

- Damaged food and beverage products
- Cracked glassware under 25 GBP
- Leaking batteries and any item showing swelling or thermal damage

## 5. Serial-number-locked products
Products with a locked serial number, such as smart home hubs in the SH-series, require a device de-registration step before replacement. The claim status shows DMG-DEREG-PENDING until de-registration completes. This step usually takes under one hour.

## 6. Repeat claims
Accounts filing more than four damage claims in a rolling 90-day period are flagged for manual review under rule DMG-RVW-04. Review does not block the current claim; it applies to subsequent claims only.
