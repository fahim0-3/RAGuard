# Sample Promotion Policy (PRO-008)
Document ID: PRO-008
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-27
Owner: Commercial Operations
Status: Synthetic evaluation policy

## 1. Voucher eligibility
One promotional voucher may be used per order. Vouchers cannot be combined with another voucher, employee discount, or price-match adjustment unless the voucher explicitly says otherwise.

Vouchers apply only to eligible products displayed in the promotion terms. Gift cards, delivery charges, extended warranties, and subscriptions are excluded from voucher discounts.

## 2. Voucher expiry and failed redemption
Each voucher has its own expiry date. An expired voucher cannot be restored, extended, or exchanged for cash. If a valid voucher fails at checkout, the customer should confirm the code, account, and eligible products before contacting support with a screenshot of the error.

## 3. Returns of discounted orders
When an item bought with a voucher is returned, the refund is calculated from the amount actually paid for that item. A voucher is not reissued after a partial return. If every eligible item from the original order is returned, a single-use voucher is still not reissued.

## 4. Promotional price adjustments
We do not refund the difference when an item goes on promotion after an order is placed. A price-match request must be made before purchase and only applies to identical products sold by approved domestic retailers.

## 5. Abuse prevention
Creating multiple accounts to reuse a new-customer voucher is prohibited. Accounts may be reviewed and promotional orders cancelled when there is evidence of voucher abuse. This review does not affect a customer's statutory rights.
