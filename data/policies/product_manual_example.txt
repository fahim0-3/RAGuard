# Product Manual: AuraBrew X200 Espresso Machine (MAN-006)
Document ID: MAN-006
Model number: AB-X200-EU
Firmware: 2.6.1
Warranty: 24 months

## 1. In the box
- AuraBrew X200 body
- 58 mm portafilter and two baskets (single, double)
- Water tank, 1.8 litres
- Cleaning tablets, pack of 4
- Water hardness test strip

## 2. First use
1. Rinse the water tank and fill to the MAX line with fresh cold water.
2. Power on using the rear switch. The ring light pulses amber while heating.
3. Run one blank cycle without coffee to prime the boiler.
4. Set the water hardness using the test strip and the menu path Settings > Water > Hardness.

Heat-up takes approximately 35 seconds from cold.

## 3. Error codes
| Code | Meaning | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | Water tank empty or not seated | Refill and reseat the tank until it clicks |
| E02 | Boiler temperature sensor fault | Power off for 10 minutes; if it repeats, the unit needs service |
| E03 | Pump over-pressure, grind too fine | Coarsen the grind and reduce the dose |
| E04 | Descale required | Run the descale cycle described in section 5 |
| E05 | Steam wand blocked | Purge the wand and clean the tip with the supplied pin |
| E07 | Firmware update interrupted | Reconnect to power and retry from the app |

## 4. Daily cleaning
Purge the group head for 2 seconds after each session. Wipe the steam wand immediately after use, then purge for 1 second. Do not immerse the portafilter handle in water.

## 5. Descaling
Descale every 3 months, or when E04 appears. Use one cleaning tablet in a full tank, then hold the two-cup button for 5 seconds to start the cycle. The cycle takes 18 minutes and must not be interrupted. Vinegar must not be used, as it voids the warranty.

## 6. Warranty and service
The 24-month warranty covers manufacturing defects. It excludes scale damage caused by skipped descaling, damage from third-party cleaning agents, and cosmetic wear. Warranty service requires the model number AB-X200-EU and the serial number printed under the drip tray.
